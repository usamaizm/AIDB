# Idea taxonomy

AIDB’s concept system is intentionally plural. It does not reduce knowledge to one structure or one mode of representation.

## Core principles

1. The structure of a record is not the same thing as its meaning.
2. A knowledge item can exist in multiple valid forms at once.
3. The same concept can appear as a scalar, a relationship, a map, or a workflow depending on context.
4. AIDB should remain flexible enough to support future structures without frozen assumptions.

## Design intent

AIDB is not trying to impose a single universal schema. Instead, it preserves a wide range of valid structures and lets the meaning emerge from context, provenance, and relationships.

This allows the system to support:

- simple values
- rich conceptual objects
- graphs and worlds
- recursive and symmetric structures
- compact and secure representations

In short: AIDB treats the world as a collection of shape-bearing ideas, each of which may carry many forms and many layers of meaning.
