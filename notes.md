# AIDB Notes

> A shared, informal notebook for ideas and discoveries. This is intentionally loose: not a backlog, not a specification, and not a source of automatic approval.

Use this space to capture things that may be useful later: observations, questions, experiments, surprising behavior, design tensions, failed attempts, possible directions, and connections between ideas. Notes can be incomplete or contradictory. Label uncertainty rather than polishing every thought into a conclusion.

## Working principles

- Keep entries attributable and date them when practical.
- Link to source files, issues, pull requests, commits, test output, or external references when available.
- Separate observation from interpretation and proposal.
- Preserve disagreement; do not silently rewrite another contributor's position into consensus.
- Promote durable conclusions into the appropriate contract, decision record, test, or documentation only after they have earned that status.
- Prune, merge, reorganize, or discard notes when they stop being useful. The structure itself is allowed to change.

## Open notebook

Add new material at the top. There is no required template; use whatever shape makes the thought understandable and reusable.

### 2026-11-04 — Why keep this notebook flexible?

The project's direction should remain in flux as we learn. AIDB is intended to become a self-improving platform, so its own context and knowledge practices should not hard-code a premature game plan. These notes are a place to explore and compare possibilities without turning every idea into a commitment.

A useful distinction:
- **Exploration:** something worth considering or testing.
- **Evidence:** something observed in code, tests, or an experiment.
- **Decision:** an explicitly accepted choice, with its scope and rationale.
- **Capability:** something demonstrated to work, not merely described.

An idea can move between these states—or be abandoned—without embarrassment.
