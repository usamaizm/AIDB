# World map

**Category:** geospatial layer / higher-order structure  
**Status:** raw / exploring

## Summary

A world map is a collection of spatial or conceptual coordinates arranged into a larger geographic or meaningful layer.

## Why it matters

AIDB can benefit from map-like organization for:

- global knowledge
- movement records
- geospatial events
- geopolitical or conceptual regions
- location-aware archives

Examples:
- migrations
- routes and regions
- global document clusters
- world-scale event overlays
