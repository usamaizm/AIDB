# x,y

**Category:** pair / coordinate  
**Status:** raw / exploring

## Summary

`x,y` is a two-part relationship or coordinate. It captures a connection between two values or points.

## Why it matters

Many relationships are not one-dimensional. AIDB benefits from preserving pairwise structure for:

- coordinates
- source-target relationships
- descriptive pairs
- correlations
- comparisons

Examples:
- `AIDB, idea`
- `origin, destination`
- `subject, object`
- `x, y` spatial coordinate
