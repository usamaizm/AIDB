# Fractal

**Category:** recursive structure  
**Status:** exploring

## Summary

A fractal is a structure that repeats itself across different scales. The same pattern can appear in small and large forms.

## Why it matters

AIDB is interested in conceptual recursion. Ideas may repeat across:

- local and global contexts
- narrow and broad abstractions
- observation and system design
- small knowledge objects and large archives

## Core idea

Fractals suggest that the same principle can recur at many levels of the system without losing identity.

Examples:
- local neighborhood patterns that repeat globally
- concepts that repeat across domains
- archive structures that resemble concept structures
- self-similar relations across scales
