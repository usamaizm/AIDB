# x,y,z

**Category:** triad / position  
**Status:** raw / exploring

## Summary

`x,y,z` adds depth. It often represents position, direction, or a three-part relation.

## Why it matters

Many real-world or conceptual structures are not just two-dimensional. This shape is useful for:

- 3D coordinates
- subject, relation, object
- object, context, outcome
- layered knowledge structures

Examples:
- `x, y, z` location
- `subject, relation, object`
- `source, mechanism, result`
