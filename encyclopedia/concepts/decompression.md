# Decompression

**Category:** restoration after compression  
**Status:** common

## Summary

Decompression restores a compressed payload back to its original or near-original form.

## Why it matters

Without decompression, compressed archives and payloads remain opaque and unusable.

## Core idea

Decompression complements compression; it is the reverse step needed to recover meaningful information.
