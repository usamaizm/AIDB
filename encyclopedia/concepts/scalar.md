# Scalar

**Category:** primitive value  
**Status:** foundational

## Summary

A scalar is a single, atomic value. It is the smallest meaningful unit of knowledge in AIDB.

## Why it matters

Scalars form the base layer for all larger structures. They can represent:

- numbers
- strings
- booleans
- identifiers
- labels
- tags
- small atomic concepts

## Core idea

A scalar is simple enough to stand alone, but it can also be enriched with metadata, provenance, and identity.

Examples:
- `42`
- `summit`
- `true`
- `task-001`
