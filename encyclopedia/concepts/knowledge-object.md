# Knowledge object

**Category:** archival unit  
**Status:** exploring

## Summary

A knowledge object is a packaged unit of meaningful information: a concept, record, artifact, or collection that can be retrieved, verified, and linked.

## Why it matters

AIDB needs a stable concept for the basic unit it preserves and references.

## Core idea

A knowledge object may include:

- identity or hash
- title or label
- summary
- content or payload
- metadata
- provenance
- related concepts
- version lineage
- access or trust information
