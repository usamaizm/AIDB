# Self-similarity

**Category:** recursive pattern  
**Status:** exploring

## Summary

Self-similarity is the repetition of a pattern at smaller or larger scales.

## Why it matters

AIDB benefits from self-similarity because knowledge often repeats in nested forms:

- a single concept can resemble a larger category
- a local cluster can resemble a global map
- a small object can reflect the shape of a larger system

## Core idea

The same pattern appears at multiple scales, offering a sense of continuity and recursive identity.

Examples:
- a local neighborhood reflecting a global structure
- nested archive collections within larger collections
- repeated concept relationships across domains
