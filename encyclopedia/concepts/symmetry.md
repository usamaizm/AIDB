# Symmetry

**Category:** invariance / equilibrium  
**Status:** exploring

## Summary

Symmetry is the preservation of structure under transformation. Something remains recognizably the same even when viewed from a different orientation or under a different coordinate system.

## Why it matters

Symmetry is useful for understanding stability, invariance, and recurring patterns in AIDB.

It can help describe:

- repeated conceptual forms
- stable structures across transformations
- transformation-safe knowledge relations
- canonical structures that remain recognizable despite variation

## Core idea

A symmetric structure preserves essential relationships even when the surface representation changes.

Examples:
- a concept remains recognizable after re-labeling
- a pattern remains consistent across scales
- a relationship holds even when coordinates shift
