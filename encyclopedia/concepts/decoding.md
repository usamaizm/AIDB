# Decoding

**Category:** representation restoration  
**Status:** common

## Summary

Decoding is the process of restoring an encoded representation to a usable format.

## Why it matters

Encoded knowledge is only useful when it can be decoded reliably and repeatedly.

## Core idea

Decoding is the inverse of encoding, though the exact operation depends on the chosen format and protocol.
