# Decryption

**Category:** access restoration  
**Status:** important

## Summary

Decryption restores encrypted content to readable or usable form using the appropriate key material.

## Why it matters

Decryption is necessary for access, review, and use of protected archives or sensitive data.

## Core idea

Decryption is the inverse of encryption and should be treated as a controlled process with clear trust assumptions.
