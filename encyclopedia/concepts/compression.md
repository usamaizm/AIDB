# Compression

**Category:** size reduction  
**Status:** common

## Summary

Compression reduces the size of a message or archive for storage or transmission.

## Why it matters

AIDB may hold large knowledge collections, logs, or artifacts. Compression helps with storage, transfer efficiency, and local-first operation.

## Core idea

Compression is primarily about reducing redundancy or expressing information more compactly.
