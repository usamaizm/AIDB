# Document

**Category:** narrative knowledge object  
**Status:** common

## Summary

A document is a narrative or descriptive body of knowledge, typically prose or markup-based content.

## Why it matters

Many knowledge systems begin as documents before becoming structured data. AIDB should support both narrative and formal structures.

## Core idea

A document may carry semantics in text, annotations, and references, and may later be decomposed into structured records.
