# Invariance

**Category:** stable property  
**Status:** exploring

## Summary

Invariance is the property of remaining unchanged under some transformation or interpretation.

## Why it matters

AIDB needs to identify which aspects of a concept remain stable even when representation changes.

This helps separate:

- core meaning from surface form
- stable structure from changing labels
- semantic continuity from transformation noise

## Core idea

A concept may change in presentation while preserving a deeper identity.

Examples:
- a record changes shape from dictionary to graph while keeping same core information
- a concept survives a change in coordinates or scale
- a meaning stays stable even when the visual representation changes
