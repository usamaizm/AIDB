# Archive

**Category:** collection / knowledge container  
**Status:** exploring

## Summary

An archive is a preserved collection of knowledge artifacts, ideas, records, or objects that should remain accessible, traceable, and revisitable.

## Why it matters

AIDB is fundamentally an archive of concepts and structures. It should preserve not just the final form of an idea, but the lineage of its evolution.

## Core idea

Archives are useful when they support:

- preservation over time
- version tracking
- retrieval and discovery
- provenance and origin
- replication or distribution
- review and revision

Examples:
- a corpus of concept entries
- a set of brainstorms
- a collection of knowledge objects
- a distributed knowledge base
