# Encoding

**Category:** representation transform  
**Status:** common

## Summary

Encoding is the process of converting information into a different representation to support compatibility, transport, or storage.

## Why it matters

AIDB may need to represent knowledge in forms such as JSON, YAML, base64, or binary encodings. Encoding is a broad transformation layer.

## Core idea

Encoding changes the format or representation of information while preserving its logical meaning, though not always the same human readability.
