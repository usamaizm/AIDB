# Super-symmetry

**Category:** higher-order relation / layered transformation  
**Status:** exploring

## Summary

Super-symmetry is a higher-order form of symmetry in which multiple layers or domains are related through deeper transformations rather than simple repeated patterns.

## Why it matters

AIDB is not only about repeated forms. It is also about relations across different layers of meaning, structure, and scale.

This can apply to:

- abstract and concrete domains
- conceptual and geometric layers
- metadata and content layers
- local and global knowledge structures
- symbolic and spatial reasoning

## Core idea

Super-symmetry recognizes that structures may not just mirror themselves; they may transform across domains while preserving relationships.

Examples:
- a graph becomes a map
- a local neighborhood becomes a global concept
- a concept changes shape while preserving its invariants
- a symbolic system aligns with a geometric one
