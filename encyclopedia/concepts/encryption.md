# Encryption

**Category:** confidentiality transform  
**Status:** important

## Summary

Encryption transforms readable data into a form that is not easily readable without the appropriate key.

## Why it matters

When AIDB stores or transmits sensitive knowledge, encryption can protect confidentiality and reduce exposure risk.

## Core idea

Encryption is not just a storage optimization; it is a trust and access-control mechanism.
